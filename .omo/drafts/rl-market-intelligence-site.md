## Decisions (with rationale)

- Use the technical value chain, not buzzwords, as primary navigation: signals/data → environments → post-training → evaluation/assurance → deployment → transformation.
- Treat RLHF/RLAIF/RLVR as feedback/reward regimes and DPO as an optimization method; do not display them as mutually exclusive peer industries.
- Use the exact canonical sourceType/control/supportRelation+derived supportSummary/confidence/temporal/risk/access/kind/independence enums and display table; never store supportSummary or compress the evidence dimensions.
- Compare exactly `vertical_domain_assurance_pack | specialist_independent_eval_lab | environment_foundry | ax_transformation_studio` without scores; only Domain Assurance Pack remains eligible after countersearch. Its separate candidates—`regulated_financial_operations | insurance_operations | healthcare_administration | enterprise_software_support | public_sector_administration`—then receive symmetric seven-dimension searches and fixed read-only scoring; no qualifying domain means no-go.
- Store each score cell's positive and negative search as distinct canonical SearchReceipts with source/observation-backed findings and closure; all 70 receipts must resolve, render in synthesis/method/sources, and pass semantic review. Missing burden/pressure is labeled `IMPUTED · CONSERVATIVE MISSING`, with unknown measured level, effective raw 5, and converted score 0.
- Use exactly seven response archetypes—`managed_data_bpo | expert_workforce_marketplace | environment_foundry_gym | eval_observability_saas | independent_assurance_lab | post_training_runtime_infrastructure | ax_transformation_consultancy`—and exactly one competitor response for each archetype plus each named company.
- Require buyer-controlled or procurement evidence before labeling a customer, contract, buying motion, or outcome confirmed; label vendor-only evidence and unknowns explicitly.
- Use exactly seven business models: `managed_data_bpo | expert_marketplace | employee_bpo | expert_environment_services | eval_observability_saas | runtime_infrastructure | proprietary_data_acquisition`; do not create a catch-all consulting model or conflate them with the seven market segments.
- Make diagrams semantic HTML/SVG with textual fallbacks, not canvas-only art.
- Treat root `SYNTHESIS.md` as a hashed seed: copy it to `research/seeds/`, map every source/factual anchor to canonical IDs or a rejection reason, generate `research/synthesis/SYNTHESIS.md`, then replace the root with a deprecation pointer. Seed recommendations remain hypotheses until scored.
- Bound the adjacent census at a minimum of 60 unique organizations across seven segment quotas with a reproducible inclusion rule and two-pass saturation stop; do not claim literal completeness.
- Use one deterministic hash grammar, `#/<section>?<query>`, with `#/showcase` as the only showcase URL; citations route to `#/sources?claim=...&source=...` and source details backlink to each claim's validated canonical hash.
- In the shared non-Git workspace, assign exclusive path owners and sequentially hand off shared entry/integration files by SHA-256; only the final integrator may combine feature modules.
