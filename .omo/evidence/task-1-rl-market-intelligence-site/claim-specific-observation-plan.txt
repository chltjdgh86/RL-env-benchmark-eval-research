Claim-specific observation remediation path (required before Task 1 can be called complete).

Current blocker
- The builder creates one observation per source and reuses it across unrelated claims. Passing structural validation therefore does not prove relationship-level entailment.

Implementation sequence
1. Replace the one-observation-per-source constructor with an observation seed ledger keyed by observationId, sourceId, exact locator, excerpt, observedAt, and validAt. Multiple observations per source are required.
2. Inventory every positive evidence relationship first. For each claim/link pair, record an entailment verdict: supports, partially_supports, context_only, contests, or contradicts.
3. Require supports/partially_supports links to point to a claim-specific excerpt and locator that contains the asserted fact or a direct primary field. Page titles, category headings, generic offer headings, and transport metadata are never positive evidence.
4. Detach generic context-only links that have no claim-specific contextual relationship, including the 144 blanket src_s47 strategy links. Keep a source in the registry without attaching it to unrelated claims.
5. Rebuild company dossiers, market analysis, strategy claims, and buyer evidence from the audited relationship ledger. Downgrade or remove any claim with no entailing observation.
6. Add a deterministic relationship-audit manifest and fixtures for one-to-many source observations, non-entailing title rejection, duplicate observation reuse, and observation/source/claim parity.
7. Render and manually inspect representative company, economics, taxonomy, and strategy claims before closing the gate.

Parallel dependency
- The 70 symmetric positive/countersearch receipts must be executed and dated separately. Until then all 35 score cells remain conservative missing values and no vertical can win.

Completion evidence

- Zero generic title/transport observations used as support.
