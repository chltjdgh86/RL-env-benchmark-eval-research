### Strategy scoring contract

- Each evidence-backed raw score is an integer 0-5. `pain`: 0 no evidence; 1 generic inconvenience; 2 documented low-consequence error; 3 measurable repeated operating/quality cost; 4 high-consequence regulatory/service impact with two aligned primary observations; 5 material safety/financial/regulatory exposure plus active budget/procurement evidence from two separate buyers. `willingnessToPay`: 0 none; 1 vendor claim only; 2 one buyer interest/pilot without spend; 3 one paid pilot/procurement or two buyer confirmations; 4 two separate paid/procurement buyers; 5 repeat/expansion or multi-year/public award evidence from at least two buyers. `rightsAccess`: 0 prohibited/unresolved blocker; 1 hypothetical access; 2 samples/synthetic only; 3 one documented lawful data/workflow path; 4 multiple lawful paths plus deployment controls; 5 durable contractual rights plus retention/refresh terms. `verifierFeasibility`: 0 no observable outcome; 1 subjective expert-only; 2 partial/gameable proxy; 3 majority state/outcome automation plus expert review; 4 strong state checks and adversarial validity evidence; 5 construct-valid multilayer verifier with false-pass/metamorphic evidence. `expertSupply`: 0 none; 1 hypothetical scarce experts; 2 one vendor-reported channel; 3 documented recruit/calibration path; 4 multiple channels or buyer experts; 5 repeatable supply with QA/utilization evidence. `freshnessBurden`: 0 stable >12 months; 1 quarterly; 2 monthly; 3 weekly; 4 daily/high-ops; 5 continuous/volatile/no reliable refresh. `incumbentPressure`: 0 no credible substitute; 1 fragmented/weak; 2 adjacent bundle requires effort; 3 multiple credible vendors/internal build; 4 hyperscaler/major incumbent bundled; 5 commodity/bundled with near-zero switching friction.
- Convert the last two effective raw levels before weighting: `freshnessScore=5-effectiveFreshnessBurden` and `competitiveWhitespaceScore=5-effectiveIncumbentPressure`; every dimension's stored/derived `convertedScore` is the score used by the formula. Missing burden/pressure never masquerades as favorable: `measuredLevel=null`, `imputation=conservative_missing`, `effectiveRawLevel=5`, and `convertedScore=0`. Formula: `rawWeightedScore = (painScore*20 + willingnessToPayScore*20 + rightsAccessScore*15 + verifierFeasibilityScore*15 + expertSupplyScore*10 + freshnessScore*10 + competitiveWhitespaceScore*10) / 5`. Keep full precision; display one decimal with `Math.round((raw+Number.EPSILON)*10)/10`.
- Every one of the 35 vertical×dimension cells runs the same positive search, countersearch, searched-domain classes, cutoff, and closure template and references exactly two of the 70 canonical SearchReceipt records even when positive evidence exists. A genuinely missing cell has converted score 0, nonblank `missingReason`, conservative imputation, and receipts stating the queries/domains/cutoff/closure; it has empty claim/observation arrays. Otherwise every cell is claim/observation backed, `imputation=null`, and `missingReason=null`. `control=subject_controlled|commercially_affiliated`, `confidence=low|unscored`, `supportSummary=contested|not_publicly_verified|contradicted`, or `temporal=superseded|unknown` caps a benefit cell at 2; one eligible independence group caps at 3. `rightsBlocker=true` if any current high-risk rights/privacy/security claim is contested/contradicted/not verified, no lawful rights observation exists, or rightsAccess <3.
- A candidate qualifies only when full-precision score >=70, all seven converted scores are >=3, it has two separate buyer/procurement observations, no rights blocker, and all nonmissing cells close. The winner is the highest-scoring qualifying vertical and must lead the second-highest candidate, qualified or not, by >=10 full-precision points. Tie order: score, willingnessToPay, rightsAccess, verifierFeasibility, freshnessScore, competitiveWhitespaceScore, then lexical `strategyId`. Otherwise no-go. The scorecard is read-only; readers cannot edit weights, cells, caps, or thresholds.

### External-link policy

- The checker follows at most five HTTPS redirects with a 15-second timeout, records raw HTTP/network result in `lastLinkCheck`, and derives canonical `access`: successful direct/redirect -> `open`; detected paywall -> `paywalled`; authentication/interstitial -> `login_gated`; lawful archive used -> `archived`; inaccessible/404/410/DNS/timeout/robots/no successful GET fallback -> `unavailable`; only an accessible corroborating/secondary page -> `secondary_only`.
- Paywalled/login-gated/unavailable sources are not automatically disproven. They need matching metadata plus accessible corroboration/archive; never bypass controls. An unavailable source cannot solely support a `current` claim and forces `unknown`, `superseded`, or `not_publicly_verified` as appropriate.
- External anchors open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`; the UI shows access state and labels archived copies. A URL resolving successfully proves availability only, never semantic support.
