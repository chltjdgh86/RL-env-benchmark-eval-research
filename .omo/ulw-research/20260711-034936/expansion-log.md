- New markers:
  - LangSmith already bundles trace clustering, evaluation, diagnosis, and improvement.

  - AWS AgentCore bundles the horizontal agent stack at consumption pricing.
  - PwC, Deloitte, and BSI occupy formal assurance/certification.

  - Gartner's cancellation forecast requires business-value qualification before selling assurance.
- Leads closed:
  - Broad horizontal Reliability Twin — refuted as an unoccupied wedge.
  - Generic browser/environment runtime — closed as a commodity rail.
  - Independence-only positioning — refuted as sufficient differentiation.
  - Generic AX studio — rejected as a primary wedge.
  - Automatic cross-client data flywheel — refuted absent explicit rights.
- Revised recommendation: narrow Domain Assurance Packs for external-evidence decisions, executed in buyer-controlled infrastructure and exported into incumbent rails; partner with formal assurance/cloud channels.
