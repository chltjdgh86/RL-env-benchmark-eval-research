# Intent vs. Reality Ledger

Core question: How did the named companies and adjacent AI data, post-training, evaluation, environment, computer-use, and AX services markets form; how do suppliers operate and sell today; and where can a focused newcomer compete?

Research axes:

1. Company origin and chronology: founding context, initial wedge, pivots, financing, milestones.
2. Current offer and customer: products/services, buyer personas, use cases, pricing clues, proof points.
3. Go-to-market motion: founder-led entry, labor supply acquisition, enterprise sales, partnerships, content, procurement and expansion.
4. Industry architecture: RLHF, RLVR, environments, computer use, evaluations, expert data, agent/AX consulting, adjacent infrastructure.
5. Unit economics and operations: supply model, workforce, quality control, delivery model, margins/cost drivers, defensibility.
6. Competitive landscape: direct competitors, substitutes, open-source/internal-build alternatives, consolidation and adjacency.
7. Newcomer strategy: underserved segments, viable wedges, differentiation, capability roadmap, risks, sequencing.
8. Evidence integrity: present-day verification, contradictions, weakly evidenced claims, and explicit unknowns.

Codebase relevant: yes, as a greenfield application target; the directory was empty at discovery. External: yes. Browsing: yes. Verification likely: yes for website behavior and source/access checks; non-code claims use the claim gate. Final format: interactive website with cited source links and diagrams.
