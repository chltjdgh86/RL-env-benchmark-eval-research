# Research Coverage and Convergence

Cutoff: 2026-07-11.

## Coverage accounting

- First wave: 14 named lane owners covering Scale, Mercor, Micro1, Turing, AfterQuery, Fleet, emerging-company dossiers, technical taxonomy, adjacent landscape, GTM, economics, buyer demand, market history, and newcomer strategy.
- Nested work: several lanes opened narrower identity, funding, customer, or countersearch specialists. The exact nested-worker total is not reconstructed, so only the 14 lane-owner floor is asserted.
- Expansion: at least two explicit waves. Wave 1 mapped the market and candidate entrant wedge; Wave 2 counter-searched the wedge against platforms, infrastructure, hyperscalers, formal assurance incumbents, buyer willingness-to-pay, data-rights constraints, and demand-quality evidence.
- Sources: `SYNTHESIS.md` retains 77 directly linked references. Lane searches consulted a larger set, but no exact deduplicated corpus total is claimed.
- Executable verifications: none for market claims. These are numeric, legal, dated, and commercial claims, so verification used primary-source preference, independent triangulation, temporal labeling, and countersearch rather than code execution.

## Convergence reason

Wave 2 did not create a new unsearched horizontal category. It converted the original broad Reliability Twin recommendation into a narrow, externally decision-relevant Domain Assurance Pack pre-score hypothesis. That candidate remains subject to the website's scorecard and no-go gate rather than being selected by the research alone. Open matters are transaction close, financing/customer metrics, private margins, and exact data rights—facts that require future filings, customer confirmation, or private access rather than another public query variant. The market is therefore converged for this deliverable, with those facts explicitly unresolved.
