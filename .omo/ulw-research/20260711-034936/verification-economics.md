# Verification Economics

| claim | risk | error cost | verification cost/time | chosen verification path | defer/verify decision | outcome | residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Named-company identities and current offers | High | Misidentifying a company invalidates its dossier. | Medium | First-party domain plus independent corroboration and counter-search. | Verify | Ten dossiers completed; Fleet/Refresh/AfterQuery/Vibrant disambiguated. | Current catalogs can drift after cutoff. |
| Funding, valuation, customer and revenue figures | High | Can materially distort competitive conclusions. | Medium to high | Primary announcement/filing plus independent domain; otherwise mark unverified. | Verify or abstain | Scale/Meta, Mercor gross run rate, Mercor/Deeptune announcement, and Deeptune A cleared; conflicting/private metrics labeled partial or unresolved. | Private financials, customers, and transaction close unavailable. |
| Industry taxonomy and value-chain mapping | Normal | Misleading market framing. | Medium | Cross-check academic/official technical sources and multiple practitioner sources. | Verify | Technical taxonomy and rows-to-worlds value chain converged. | Fast-moving terminology; RLVR commercial usage remains loose. |
| Newcomer strategy recommendations | High | Could drive costly decisions. | High | Tie each recommendation to supported market observations and state inference explicitly. | Verify | Broad Reliability Twin refuted; conditional Domain Assurance Pack retained after countersearch. | WTP and repeatability require paid field validation. |
| Frontend behavior and accessibility | Normal | Broken deliverable. | Low to medium | Automated tests, production build, live browser use at multiple widths. | Verify | Pending | Pending |
