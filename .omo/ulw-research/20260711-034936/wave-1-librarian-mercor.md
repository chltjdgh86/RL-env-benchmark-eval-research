# Wave 1: Mercor Dossier

Observer: `/root/mercor_dossier` · completed 2026-07-11.

## Key findings

- Mercor was legally incorporated in 2022 and began operating in January 2023 as an automated cross-border recruiting marketplace. Its AI interviewing, matching, contracting and payment stack became the production engine for expert-created model data.
- By February 2025 Mercor publicly named frontier-AI expert contracting as its focus. Its 2025-2026 product expansion added APEX benchmarks, off-the-shelf datasets, enterprise workflow capture/agent deployment, and RL environments.
- The current product surface is Work, Hire, Evaluate, and Build. Recruiting remains live, but data/evaluation/environment work is the strategic center.
- The last closed financing is the October 2025 $350M Series C at a $10B valuation. July 2026 $500M/$20B reports are talks, not a closed round.
- Reported $1B-$2B annualized revenue is gross marketplace revenue. The Information reported 60-70% paid to contractors and $300-$400M net revenue at the $1B gross level. Current primary payout figures are directionally consistent.
- Network size, evaluated users, roles created, cumulative hires, and weekly active experts are different measures; five million network profiles do not mean five million paid workers.
- OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta relationships are independently reported historically. Meta paused work after the March 2026 security incident; Mercor's claim that all labs later expanded is not independently confirmed.
- On July 9, 2026 Mercor announced an agreement to acquire Deeptune; price and legal closing status remain unknown.
- Material risks include customer concentration, security and resulting litigation, worker-classification allegations, contractor volatility, metric optics, and limited independent evidence for the newer enterprise-agent product.
