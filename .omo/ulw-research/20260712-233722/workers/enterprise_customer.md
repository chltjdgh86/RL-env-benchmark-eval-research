Countersearch exclusions worth recording:

- InsureBench is announced for August 2026 but currently exposes no downloadable cases, so it is not yet an available sample dataset.
- Beyond IVR has a benchmark paper but no canonical public data release found.
- NOWAI-Bench is a directory page pointing to EnterpriseOps-Gym and EVA-Bench, not a third dataset.
- The three τ releases are not interchangeable: original τ-bench is stale, Sierra’s current repo is τ³-bench, and Amazon’s Verified fork is a separate corrected snapshot.
- Several Hugging Face mirrors and personal reuploads were excluded in favor of the publisher-owned canonical dataset.
