Rejected after counter-search:

- `General-AgentBench` is primarily a unified runner over reused benchmarks, with no distinct task dataset verified.
- `OmniBench` claims 36k synthesized tasks, but its repository still marks public test-task release as TODO.
- `TRIP-Bench`, `LiveAgentBench`, and `AgentDrive` surfaced papers but no canonical downloadable task release.
- `TOBench/MM-ToolBench` currently links to a nonexistent `AI-Safeguard/TOBenchURL`; no usable canonical dataset could be verified.
- Pure leaderboard/result repositories were excluded unless they exposed prompts, inputs, traces, or task artifacts.

## CLAIMS

- Broad ecosystem-first search adds more than 35 verified task or trajectory releases beyond the user’s three seed URLs.
- The strongest general-agent data families are AgencyBench, AgentGym, GAIA/OmniGAIA, AssistantBench, planning datasets, OpenClaw benchmark families, ALE, EdgeBench, and Agentick.
- Access must be modeled explicitly as `open`, `gated`, or `partial-sample`; several important corpora expose only a subset or hide references.
- Benchmark tasks and trajectory datasets should be separate dataset types in the site because many publishers release both.
